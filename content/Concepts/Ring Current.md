# Ring Current

A toroidal current system in Earth's inner magnetosphere ($L \approx 2$–$9\ R_E$) flowing westward. Carries energetic trapped particles — primarily H$^+$, O$^+$, and He$^+$ ions (10–200 keV) as well as electrons — that undergo gradient and curvature drift around the Earth. The ring current depresses Earth's surface equatorial magnetic field; the standard proxy is the **SYM-H** (or Dst) index, which decreases by tens to hundreds of nT during the main phase of geomagnetic storms.

## Particle drifts and asymmetry

In a dipole-like field, charged particles undergo energy-dependent gradient and curvature drifts:

- **Ions** (positive charge): drift westward (dusk side)
- **Electrons** (negative charge): drift eastward (dawn side)

During storm main phase, impulsive convection electric fields inject plasma from the plasma sheet into the inner magnetosphere. The ions and electrons are transported inward primarily by the $\mathbf{E}\times\mathbf{B}$ drift and then follow their respective gradient/curvature drift paths. Because the ring current builds up preferentially on the **dusk side** (ions injected from the nightside drift westward toward dusk), the pressure distribution is asymmetric — a **partial ring current** develops before the energy is fully redistributed around the dusk-dawn arc.

## Connection to SAPS and Region-2 currents

The ring current's pressure gradient distorts magnetic field lines at the dusk edge of the inner magnetosphere and closes through the ionosphere as **Region-2 field-aligned currents** (downward on the dusk side). These Region-2 FACs establish the **Sub-Auroral Polarization Streams (SAPS)** electric field in the ionosphere by generating a poleward electric field between the low-latitude boundary of diffuse electron precipitation and the plasmapause (see [[Subauroral Polarization Streams]]).

[[Bao 2023 Geospace Plume MAGE]] demonstrates with MAGE simulations that the **spatial distributions of ring current ions and electrons** — controlled by their energy-dependent gradient/curvature drifts — are the intrinsic cause of SAPS. Hot ring current protons accumulate at the sunward azimuthal edge (distorting field lines and generating Region-2 current), while the source region of diffuse electron precipitation (hot electrons) partially overlaps with the Region-2 current's low-latitude boundary. This energy-dependent drift balance self-consistently maintains the SAPS electric field throughout storm main phase.

## Geospace plume control

The ring current indirectly controls the **geospace plume** (combined ionospheric SED plume + plasmaspheric drainage plume) by setting the SAPS electric field that erodes the dusk edge of the plasmasphere and drives sunward transport in the sub-auroral ionosphere. See [[Tongue of Ionization]], [[Storm-Enhanced Density]], and [[Subauroral Polarization Streams]] for downstream effects.

## Decay

The ring current decays during storm recovery on timescales of hours to days through:
- Charge exchange with cold geocoronal H atoms (primarily for 10–100 keV O$^+$/H$^+$)
- Wave-particle interactions (EMIC waves scatter particles to the loss cone)
- Coulomb collisions
