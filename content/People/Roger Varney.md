# Roger Varney (R. H. Varney)

UCLA Department of Atmospheric and Oceanic Sciences. [[Michael Lundquist]]'s PhD advisor. Research line: ionospheric modeling, plasma transport, and [[RISR-N]] / RISR-C science — including patch-particle precipitation interactions, polar-cap density structure, and supplemental work on the Ionospheric Polar Wind Model (IPWM, see Varney 2014).

## PhD Background

Cornell University PhD, 2012. Advisor: Prof. Michael Kelley (the founding figure of US incoherent scatter radar science and equatorial ionospheric electrodynamics). Dissertation: *"Photoelectron Transport and Energy Balance in the Low-Latitude Ionosphere"* (see [[Varney 2012 Thesis]]). The thesis develops [[SAMI2]]-PE — an extension of the NRL SAMI2 model with a self-consistent Boltzmann-Fokker-Planck photoelectron transport solver — and validates it against full-profile electron temperature measurements at the [[Jicamarca Radio Observatory]].

Post-PhD: Jack Eddy Fellow (NSF/UCAR postdoctoral fellowship in heliophysics). CEDAR Science Steering Committee (CSSC) service. Now faculty at UCLA AOS.

## Research

Current focus at UCLA: high-latitude ionospheric modeling, O⁺ ion chemistry and transport at polar latitudes ([[RISR-N]] statistics, backtracers, patches), and the IPWM/HIDRA model development. PI on PRISM grant (CEDAR program; Atlas/Papers/Grants/ has the proposal). The polar-cap density structure work in [[Lundquist Varney 2026]] and Varney 2026 PatchesChapter builds on the ionospheric modeling expertise developed in the Cornell thesis.
