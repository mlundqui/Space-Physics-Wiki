# Bellan 2006 Fundamentals of Plasma Physics

> **Version caveat:** The PDF was created in October 2004, before the 2006 Cambridge edition. It may be a pre-publication draft, so section and equation numbers cited here are **those of the vault PDF** and may differ from the print edition.

## Summary

A graduate plasma-physics text. Only the sections used by the derivation pages have been read so far: §1.6 (Debye shielding), Ch. 2 (Vlasov equation, moments, two-fluid and MHD equations, frozen-in flux), §3.3–3.6 (adiabatic invariants, drift equations, mirrors, $J$, $\Phi$), and §4.3 (MHD waves).

## Key claims

- The general proof that the action of a slowly changing periodic motion is invariant (§3.4.1).
- The MHD fast/slow dispersion quartic and its roots (Eqs. 4.84–4.85).
- The shear wave is labeled the "MHD shear (slow) mode" (§4.3.7). This differs from the space-physics convention, which calls it the intermediate mode; see [[MHD Wave Modes]].

## Methods/data

Analytical. SI units.
