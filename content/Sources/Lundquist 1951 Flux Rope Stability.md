# Lundquist 1951 Flux Rope Stability

**Citation:** Lundquist, S. (1951), *On the Stability of Magneto-Hydrostatic Fields*, Phys. Rev., 83(2), 307–309.

## Summary

Mathematical treatment of the stability of static magnetic fields in an electrically conducting liquid (ideal MHD). For force-free cylindrical magnetic configurations (curlH × H = 0, equivalently curlH = αH), the exact solution for an infinite cylinder is H_z = AJ₀(αr), H_φ = AJ₁(αr), where J₀ and J₁ are Bessel functions of the first kind. Lundquist demonstrates that the kink instability sets in when the magnetic energy added by twisting the cylinder equals the energy of the original untwisted field. This is the foundational result for magnetic flux rope stability, now understood as a key trigger condition for solar eruptions (CMEs) and geomagnetic tail flux ropes.

## Key claims

- Force-free fields satisfy curlH × H = 0; equivalently J × B = 0 — no volumetric force on the fluid; can exist in compressible media where pressure gradients vanish.
- Cylindrical force-free solution: H_z = AJ₀(αr), H_φ = AJ₁(αr), where α = curlH/H = constant; this is the "Lundquist field."
- Ideal MHD (σ → ∞): E + v×B = 0; magnetic flux through any surface moving with the fluid is conserved; field lines are frozen into the fluid.
- **Kink instability threshold:** the configuration becomes unstable when the magnetic energy contributed by twisting reaches the energy of the original untwisted field — i.e., when the twist-to-length ratio (field line helicity per unit length) crosses a critical value. Below this threshold the configuration is stable.
- Physical mechanism: twist energy is stored in the toroidal field component H_φ; the kink mode releases this energy by allowing the flux tube to buckle.

## Methods/data

- Purely theoretical MHD stability analysis; stability criterion derived by evaluating the sign of the second variation of the magnetic energy under ideal MHD perturbations.

## Connections

## Open questions

- How does the infinite-cylinder result generalize to finite-length coronal loops with photospheric boundary conditions? (The Kruskal-Shafranov criterion addresses this case.)
- What role do Lundquist-type flux ropes play in geomagnetic substorm onset vs. flux-rope-associated CME impacts on the ionosphere?
