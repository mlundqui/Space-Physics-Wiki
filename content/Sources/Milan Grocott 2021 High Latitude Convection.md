# Milan Grocott 2021 High Latitude Convection

*High Latitude Ionospheric Convection.* Milan, S.E. & Grocott, A. (2021). In C. Huang & G. Lu (Eds.), *Ionosphere Dynamics and Applications*, Geophysical Monograph 260, Ch. 2, pp. 21–42. DOI:10.1002/9781119815617.ch2.

## Summary

Comprehensive review of high-latitude ionospheric convection: how solar wind energy enters the magnetosphere-ionosphere system through magnetic reconnection, drives the Dungey cycle, and produces the two-cell convection pattern observed in the ionosphere. Covers steady-state theory, time-varying expanding/contracting polar cap model, current systems, and the relationship to substorms.

## Key Claims

**Steady-state convection:**
- Twin-cell pattern with antisunward flow across polar cap and sunward return flows at lower latitudes; quantified by the cross-polar-cap potential (CPCP).
- CPCP ∝ solar wind Mach number, dawn-dusk electric field, and dayside reconnection rate; empirically 30–100 kV during typical conditions, up to 150–250 kV during super-storms.
- IMF B_z controls magnitude; IMF B_y drives dawn-dusk asymmetry (moves the convection pattern toward dawn/dusk, creating crescent/offset cells).
- Region 1 FACs close through the magnetopause/cusp region; Region 2 FACs close through the ring current / partial ring current.

**Time-varying: expanding/contracting polar cap (ECPC) model:**
- Dayside reconnection rate Φ_D adds open flux → polar cap expands, auroral oval moves equatorward.
- Nightside reconnection rate Φ_N removes open flux → polar cap contracts, oval moves poleward.
- When Φ_D > Φ_N: unbalanced expansion; when Φ_N > Φ_D: contraction (substorm onset condition).
- Substorm cycle embedded in ECPC as episodic storage (Φ_D > Φ_N during growth phase) and release (Φ_N surge during expansion) of open flux.

**Ionosphere-atmosphere coupling:**
- Neutral winds from ion-neutral momentum exchange partially offset and rotate the ionospheric convection pattern (Joule heating and sub-Alfvénic feedback).
- Atmospheric drag reduces effective convection speed; important for accurate CPCP estimates.

**Observational context:**
- Statistical convection models (Thomas & Shepherd 2018; Weimer; Ruohoniemi & Greenwald) show 9-panel IMF-dependence (Fig. 2.1); CPCP ranges 21–91 kV across IMF orientations at fixed solar wind electric field 3–20 mV/m.
- IMAGE satellite, AMPERE (66 Iridium satellites), SuperDARN provided observations for time-varying picture.
