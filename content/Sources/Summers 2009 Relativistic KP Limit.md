# Summers 2009 Relativistic KP Limit

*Limit on stably trapped particle fluxes in planetary magnetospheres.* Summers, D., Tang, R., & Thorne, R. M. (2009). *Journal of Geophysical Research*, 114, A10210. doi:10.1029/2009JA014428.

## Summary

Fully relativistic extension of the Kennel-Petschek (K-P) self-limiting flux theory for trapped energetic particles in planetary radiation belts. Replaces the original K-P wave-reflection criterion with the condition that electromagnetic waves generated at the magnetic equator acquire a specified convective gain over a given growth length (marginal stability). Derives relativistic formulae for both the limiting integral flux $I_{4\pi}$ and differential flux at a given $L$ shell, and compares theoretical limits against observed electron fluxes at Earth, Jupiter, and Uranus. Authors include Richard Thorne (UCLA).

## Key Claims

1. **Relativistic effects significantly modify K-P trapping limits** at electron energies > 100 keV, especially in low-density regions; nonrelativistic limits overestimate the allowable flux.
2. **Marginal stability replaces wave reflection** as the wave-growth condition: waves generated at the equator with convective gain $G$ over growth length $\ell$ reach the limit rather than requiring reflection at low altitudes (as in Kennel 1966).
3. **Limiting flux depends explicitly on spectral index $l$ and pitch-angle index $s$** of the assumed power-law particle distribution, and on the ratio $|\Omega_e|/\omega_{pe}$ (cold plasma parameter $a$).
4. **Comparison with observed fluxes at Earth** ($5 \leq L \leq 10$): observed values are generally slightly below the K-P limit — consistent with flux self-regulation.
5. **Jupiter and Uranus:** limiting flux calculations match observed Voyager measurements within observational/theoretical uncertainties, suggesting K-P self-regulation is universal in planetary magnetospheres.

## Methods/Data

- Analytical: relativistic distribution function $f(p_\parallel, p_\perp)$; temporal growth rate $\omega_i$ for field-aligned R-mode (whistler) waves in relativistic plasma.
- Convective gain condition: $G = \omega_i \ell / v_g$ where $v_g$ is group velocity along field line; replaces Kennel 1966 wave-reflection-loss criterion.
- Comparison data: Earth (historical surveys), Jupiter (Voyager 2 LECP), Uranus (Voyager 2 LECP).
