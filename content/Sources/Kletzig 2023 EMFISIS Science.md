# Kletzig 2023 EMFISIS Science

**Citation:** Kletzing, C. A., et al. (2023), *The Electric and Magnetic Fields Instrument Suite and Integrated Science (EMFISIS): Science, Data, and Usage Best Practices*, Space Sci. Rev., 219, 28, doi:10.1007/s11214-023-00973-z.

## Summary

Post-mission science and data assessment for EMFISIS on the Van Allen Probes (2012–2019). Synthesizes key scientific results across all measured wave modes, documents data products (L2–L4) and known calibration issues, and provides guidance for correct use of derived products — in particular the wave-normal analysis (WNA) which yields polarization, ellipticity, and Poynting flux, and the plasma density derived from the HFR upper hybrid line. Over 500 refereed papers resulted from EMFISIS data.

## Key claims

- **ULF waves (few mHz–few Hz):** Solar wind dynamic pressure and shear velocity fluctuations drive compressional and toroidal ULF modes; outward radial diffusion during storm main phase transports outer-belt electrons to the magnetopause for permanent loss; inward diffusion during recovery rebuilds outer belt at L ~ 5.
- **Chorus (0.2–0.6 Ω_e, outside plasmapause):** Fresh substorm electron injections with T_⊥ > T_∥ drive chorus wave growth; local acceleration by chorus produces enhancement peaks at L ~ 5; inward diffusion + local acceleration together explain outer belt recovery events.
- **Plasmaspheric hiss (100 Hz–2 kHz, inside plasmapause):** Primary mechanism for slow (hours–days) pitch-angle scattering of electrons into the loss cone; controls slot region (L ~ 2–3) formation and outer belt lower-energy boundary.
- **EMIC waves (near ion cyclotron frequency, dusk–dawn):** Proton ring current injection drives EMIC; causes rapid (minutes–hours) precipitation of relativistic electrons (E > ~2 MeV); most effective at L > 3 during storm main phase.
- **Magnetosonic (equatorial) noise:** Observed near the magnetic equator; bounce-resonant electron acceleration possible.
- **Plasma density from HFR:** Upper hybrid frequency f_UH² = f_p² + f_ce²; most accurate density estimate available, extending to densities where particle instruments saturate; used as boundary condition for wave propagation models.
- **WNA guidance:** L4 wave-normal angle, polarization, ellipticity, and Poynting flux products require careful gain/phase calibration of WFR channels; uncalibrated use leads to systematic errors in wave-normal angle.

## Methods/data

- Retrospective review of the full Van Allen Probes mission (2012–2019); synthesizes published results from >500 papers using EMFISIS data.
- Data availability: L2–L4 products publicly available through NASA CDAWeb; L4 WNA and density products are derived, not raw.

## Open questions

- Relative importance of local chorus acceleration vs. inward radial diffusion for producing MeV outer belt electron enhancements — still debated.
- Role of nonlinear wave-particle interactions (phase trapping, chorus holes) relative to quasi-linear diffusion.
