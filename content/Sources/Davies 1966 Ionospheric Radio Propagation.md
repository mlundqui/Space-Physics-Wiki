# Davies 1966 Ionospheric Radio Propagation

*Ionospheric Radio Propagation*, K. Davies (1966; title and author from the vault PDF; publisher not checked).

## Summary

A classic text on HF ionospheric propagation and ionosondes. So far only §2.3 (magneto-ionic theory: derivation and properties of the Appleton formula) has been read, for [[Appleton-Hartree Equation]].

## Key claims (§2.3)

- The Appleton formula is derived from Maxwell's equations plus electron constitutive relations, for a cold, uniform, electron-only medium with energy-independent collisions. Hartree's Lorentz-polarization term is "unjustified" by most evidence (§2.3.2).
- At normal incidence the O-wave reflects at $X = 1$, and the X-wave at $X = 1 - Y$ ($f > f_H$) or $X = 1 + Y$ ($f < f_H$) (Eqs. 2.80–2.81).
- QT/QL approximations, and their limited validity (§2.3.3).
