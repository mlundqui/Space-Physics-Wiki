# Thorne 1993 AOS 250B Course Reader

> **Scope note:** This is a course reader (UCLA AOS 250B, © 1993 Richard M. Thorne). Course PDFs are normally out of the wiki's scope. This one is cited because Michael explicitly asked for course readers to be used as derivation sources (MEMORY.md, 2026-10-07). It was read earlier for the course-seeded concept pages [[Radiation Belts]], [[Plasma Waves]], [[Wave-Particle Interactions]] and [[Synchrotron Radiation]], but had no source page until now. It uses **Gaussian units** and relativistic notation throughout.

## Summary

A 126-page reader on the physics of energetic charged particles in planetary magnetic fields. Ch. 1 covers orbital dynamics (guiding-center drifts, the three adiabatic invariants, mirroring, Fermi and betatron acceleration, bounce and drift periods in a dipole, loss cones, convection electric fields, drift-shell splitting). Later chapters cover incoherent radiation (Ch. 2), collective effects and the Vlasov equation (Ch. 3), cold-plasma waves (Ch. 4), and wave–particle interactions and pitch-angle diffusion.

## Key claims (Ch. 1, as used in the derivations)

- All drifts written relativistically. The general guiding-center drift is Eq. 1.27, with gradient and curvature terms weighted $\tfrac12\sin^2\alpha$ and $\cos^2\alpha$ (Eq. 1.22).
- The invariant is $p_\perp^2/B$, not $\mu$, for relativistic particles (p. 14).
- Bounce period $\tau_b = (4r_0/v)F(\lambda_m)$, with $F(0) = \pi/3\sqrt2\approx0.74$, $F(\pi/2)\approx1.38$, and $F\approx1.30-0.56\sin\alpha_0$ (Eqs. 1.30–1.30a).
- Dipole loss cone $\sin^2\alpha_L = L^{-3}(4-3/L)^{-1/2}$ (Eq. 1.31a).
- Table 6.1 gives gyration, bounce and drift periods for protons and electrons at several $L$ and mirror latitudes.
- Electric-field drifts dominate below about 1 keV; $\nabla B$ drifts dominate at higher energy (p. 17).
