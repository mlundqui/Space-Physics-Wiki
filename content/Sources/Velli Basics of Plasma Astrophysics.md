# Velli Basics of Plasma Astrophysics

*Chiuderi, C., and M. Velli (2015),* Basics of Plasma Astrophysics, *UNITEXT for Physics, Springer-Verlag Italia, Milan. ISBN 978-88-470-5279-6.* The vault filename credits Velli alone; the book has two authors.

## Summary

A graduate text on MHD and plasma astrophysics, in **Gaussian units**. So far only Ch. 9 (magnetic reconnection) has been read, for the [[Sweet-Parker Reconnection]] derivation.

## Key claims

- **Sweet–Parker (§9.1.1).** Inflow equals diffusion ($v_i = \eta/\delta$), mass balance ($Lv_i = \delta v_o$) and outflow at the inflow Alfvén speed give a rate $R_i = v_i/c_{ai} = S^{-1/2}$, with $\delta = LS^{-1/2}$ and $B_o = B_iS^{-1/2}$ (Eqs. 9.8–9.11).
- **Pressure gradients along the sheet** modify the outflow: $v_o^2 = 2c_{ai}^2 + 2(P_i - P_o)/\rho$ (Eq. 9.12). A high exit pressure lowers $v_o$ and raises the rate above $S^{-1/2}$.
- **Petschek (§9.1.2).** Slow shocks standing off the diffusion region give a maximum rate $\propto(\ln S)^{-1}$. For $\gamma = 5/3$, 3/5 of the incoming magnetic energy becomes kinetic energy and 2/5 becomes heat. Resistive simulations reproduce it only with localized anomalous resistivity.
- **Hall reconnection.** The Hall term (through whistler waves) gives reconnection faster than Sweet–Parker once the sheet thins to ion scales.
- **Plasmoid instability (§9.2).** Sweet–Parker sheets are tearing-unstable at a rate that grows with $S$. Laminar sheets can't exceed an aspect ratio of about $S^{1/3}$ (Pucci & Velli 2013).
