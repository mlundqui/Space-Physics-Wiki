# Claudepierre 2022 Radiation Belt Losses

*Quantifying Radiation Belt Electron Loss Processes at L < 4.* Claudepierre, S.G., Ma, Q., & Bortnik, J. (2022). *Journal of Geophysical Research: Space Physics*, 127, e2022JA030756. doi:10.1029/2022JA030756.

## Summary

Comprehensive quasilinear analysis of pitch-angle scattering loss rates for 30 keV–4 MeV electrons at L < 4 during geomagnetically quiet times, considering hiss waves, lightning-generated whistler (LGW) waves, VLF transmitter waves, EMIC waves, and Coulomb collisions with neutral particles. Finds that Coulomb energy drag is critically important at L ≤ 2 and that plasmaspheric density model choice strongly affects decay timescales. UCLA paper (Claudepierre/Ma/Bortnik).

## Key Claims

1. **At L ≤ 2, Coulomb energy drag (ionization energy loss)** is an important loss process that must not be neglected — including it significantly improves quantitative agreement with observed decay timescales.
2. **Electron decay timescales are very sensitive to the choice of plasmaspheric density model** — different density assumptions (plasmaspheric models) can change predicted lifetimes by orders of magnitude at the same L and energy.
3. **Including LGW transmitters improves quantitative agreement** with observed lifetimes at L ≈ 1.8–3.2.
4. **Fokker-Planck pitch-angle diffusion** computed from empirical wave amplitude/frequency/normal angle spectra; compared to in-situ Van Allen Probe exponential decay timescales.
5. **Drift-loss-cone correction** (using accurate geomagnetic field model rather than dipole): can modify theoretically calculated lifetimes significantly at large L.
6. Overall, the model reproduces observed e-folding timescales (~30 keV–4 MeV) well across most of L = 1–4 when Coulomb drag is included.

## Methods/Data

- Empirical wave parameterizations for hiss, LGW, VLF transmitters, EMIC waves from published spectral models.
- Quasilinear diffusion coefficients → Fokker-Planck pitch-angle diffusion equation; eigenvalue decomposition for lifetimes.
- Van Allen Probe flux measurements for observed e-folding times (from Claudepierre et al. 2020a, companion study).
- Accurate Tsyganenko + IGRF magnetic field model for drift-loss cone.

## Open Questions

- Nonlinear interactions (chorus, highly oblique waves) contribute at L > 4 and during active times — outside this paper's scope.
- EMIC wave contribution to electron loss at L < 4 is poorly constrained due to sparse direct wave observations there.
