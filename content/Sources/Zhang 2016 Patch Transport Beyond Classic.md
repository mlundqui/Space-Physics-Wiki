# Zhang 2016 Patch Transport Beyond Classic

*Polar cap patch transportation beyond the classic scenario.* Zhang, Q.-H., et al. (2016). *Journal of Geophysical Research: Space Physics*, 121, 9063–9075. doi:10.1002/2016JA022443.

## Summary

Documents the continuous lifecycle of a single polar cap patch from SED fragmentation through stagnation and decay — a trajectory that departs entirely from the classic antisunward-transport scenario. A substorm-generated SAPS segmented the SED/TOI plume to produce the patch, but subsequent northward IMF turning halted antisunward transport and the patch evolved in a reverse (lobe) convection cell in the afternoon sector before rapid density decay.

## Key Claims

1. **Patch formed from SED by SAPS-driven segmentation** during substorm recovery — not by cusp reconnection dynamics as in the dominant Lockwood & Carlson 1992 mechanism.
2. **Initial antisunward motion halted by IMF northward turning**: B_z reversed from strongly southward to strongly eastward/weakly northward during substorm recovery; reverse convection cell formed in afternoon sector.
3. **Patch stagnated and evolved in a lobe reverse convection cell** in the duskside afternoon sector, trapped behind the open-closed boundary rather than convecting across the polar cap.
4. **Much of the patch faded rapidly**: enhanced ionization recombination rate (due to slow neutral wind allowing ions to settle to lower altitudes with higher neutral collision frequency) destroyed the patch density enhancement faster than normal.
5. **Classic scenario**: patch transported antisunward along twin-cell streamlines from dayside to nightside in ~1 hour.
6. **Beyond-classic**: lobe reconnection (stirred lobe cell) in afternoon sectors can prevent, slow, or reverse antisunward patch transport; patch lifetime then determined by local chemistry rather than transit time.
7. Three patch-segmentation mechanism families in literature: (1) IMF regulation of cusp convection pattern; (2) plasma depletion in newly-opened flux channels; (3) transient reconnection (now considered dominant in European sector). Substorm SAPS segmentation adds a fourth mechanism.

## Methods/Data

- Multi-instrument event study: January 2014 (specific date not extracted).
- SuperDARN global convection maps (2-min cadence, map potential technique with DMSP supplement).
