# Akbari 2014 NEIAL Aspect Angle

## Summary

Uses PFISR (Poker Flat ISR; 65.1°N, 147.5°W) multibeam capability to systematically study the magnetic aspect angle dependence of [[NEIALs]] during substorm expansion phases. Two experiments (1 March 2011 with 5 beams at 1° separation; 23 March 2007 with a 3×3 beam array). Key finding: NEIALs show extremely strong aspect angle dependence — Type 2 echoes above the F-region peak virtually disappear at offsets > 2° from the magnetic zenith — allowing classification of NEIAL generation mechanisms by their angular sensitivity.

## Key claims

- NEIAL power in the upper F region (> 250 km) decreases sharply with angle; echoes are almost completely absent at 2° offset from the magnetic zenith, confirming the source is not due to spatial inhomogeneity in the NEIAL source.
- **Two spectral types** defined: Type 1 (filled ion line, zero-Doppler central peak, weak angle dependence → parametric decay of Langmuir waves) and Type 2 (enhanced ion acoustic shoulders, strong angle dependence, Doppler shift of ~−2 kHz indicating upward plasma velocity of ~660 m/s → ion-ion two-stream instability).
- Type 2 NEIALs above ~200 km are inconsistent with parametric Langmuir decay: the wide altitude extent (150–600+ km), simultaneous shoulder enhancement, and upward plasma motion all argue against Langmuir decay and in favor of ion-ion streaming.
- Below ~200 km, Type 2 echoes show weaker aspect angle sensitivity — consistent with partial ion demagnetization (more ion-neutral collisions), suggesting the effective generation mechanism may differ at low altitudes.
- Multiple generation mechanisms (Langmuir decay and ion-ion streaming) can operate simultaneously during dynamic substorm conditions.

## Methods / data

- PFISR (450 MHz, electronically steerable AMISR): multibeam sampling at 1° and ~1.4°–7.3° magnetic offset angles
- All-sky camera at 557.7 nm (O atom emission; 120–180 km) as proxy for electron precipitation
- Substorm expansion phase identified by poleward-expanding auroral forms
- Occurrence rate metric: fraction of pulses with $\geq 10\times$ thermal power at each altitude

## Open questions

- What is the NEIAL generation mechanism below 200 km? Ion cyclotron heating, downward-streaming electrons, or a third mechanism?
- Are there NEIAL events at RISR-N (polar cap) distinct from those at PFISR (auroral zone)?
- Can NEIAL spectral type and aspect angle observations be used quantitatively to infer electron precipitation flux?
